  <action>
Add `--status-settled` and `--status-settled-bg` tokens in three places in globals.css. This is a three-place edit — all three locations must be updated or Tailwind utility classes will not generate.

**Place 1 — `:root` block** (after `--status-split-bg` around line 127):
```css
--status-settled:            oklch(0.48 0.21 145);
--status-settled-bg:         oklch(0.94 0.05 145);
```
Rationale: hue 145 (green) matching open, but chroma 0.21 vs open's 0.18 and lightness 0.48 vs open's 0.50 — this makes settled visually distinct (slightly more saturated, fractionally darker) and reads as a "completed/terminal" state rather than "available."

**Place 2 — `.dark` block** (after `--status-split-bg` around line 183):
```css
--status-settled:            oklch(0.68 0.19 145);
--status-settled-bg:         oklch(0.22 0.06 145);
```
Rationale: dark open is 0.70/0.16/145; settled at 0.68/0.19/145 — slightly darker lightness and higher chroma to distinguish.

**Place 3 — `@theme inline` block** (after `--color-status-split-bg` around line 79):
```css
--color-status-settled:    var(--status-settled);
--color-status-settled-bg: var(--status-settled-bg);
```
This exposes `bg-status-settled-bg`, `text-status-settled`, and `border-status-settled` as Tailwind utility classes.

Do NOT add literal OKLCH values to `@theme inline` — only `var(--token)` references are allowed (dark mode breaks silently otherwise).
